Employer Quick Guide

Tap a task to open it

Everything you need to post opportunities, hire students, and support them on the job. Questions about the program? Reach out to your partner school.

1Sign in
I'm an Employer→Log in
  • Enter your email and password, then tap Send Login Code.
  • Check your email for a 6-digit code and enter it to finish signing in.
  • New to Starteryou? Use the invite link from your partner school to create your account.
2Find your way around
Dashboard
  • Your Dashboard shows your active jobs and total applicants. Tap Post a Job to add a new opportunity.
  • The bottom bar has Jobs, Projects, Events, Placements and Clearance.
  • Messages live under the speech bubble in the top right.
3Review applicants
Jobs→Open your posting→Scroll to applicants
  • Each applicant card shows the student's answer to “Why should I hire you?”
  • Use the buttons on each card: Message to reach out, Schedule interview to set up a call, Hire candidate to send an offer, or Reject if it's not a fit.
  • Filter by All, Shortlisted or Offered. Try to respond to every applicant within a few days.
4Schedule an interview
Applicant card→Schedule interview
  • Pick a start time and length, then paste your Zoom, Google Meet, Teams or Webex link.
  • Tap Schedule Interview. The student is notified and sees it in their Interviews.
5Make an offer
Applicant card→Hire candidate
  • Add a proposed start date and a short welcome message. Every field is optional.
  • Tap Send Offer. Once the student accepts, they move to your Placements.

Check with the school before the student's first day. Many schools have paperwork to finish before a student can start.

6Verify hours (weekly)
Placements→Work Logs
  • Students log their hours after each shift. Open Work Logs on their card to verify or reject each entry.
  • Try to review entries within a few days so the school's records stay up to date.
  • The cards at the top show your active students, approved hours, evaluations due and pending logs.
7Evaluate your student
Placements→Evaluate
  • When the school asks for an evaluation, choose the evaluation period and rate each area from 1 to 5.
  • Add a short comment. Specific feedback helps the student grow.
8Messages
Speech bubble (top right)
  • Schools is for your partner schools. Students is for applicants and interns, and you can filter by job.
  • Keep conversations with students in Starteryou, not by personal text or social media. The school can see these conversations.

Remember

  • Respond quickly. Students are often applying for their first job, and a quick reply goes a long way.
  • Stay on Starteryou. Keep messages, interviews and offers in the app so the school stays in the loop.
  • Safety first. If you have a concern about a student or your site, contact the school right away.
