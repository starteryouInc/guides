Student Quick Guide

Tap a task to open it

Everything you need to find an opportunity, get hired, and track your hours. Stuck? Ask your school coordinator.

1Sign in
I'm a Student→Sign In
  • Sign in with your Microsoft or Google school account, or with the email and password from your school's invite.
  • Don't tap “Create an account.” Your school already set you up. If you can't get in, tell your coordinator.
  • Prefer Spanish? Menu → Settings → Español
2Find opportunities
Jobs→School Jobs
  • School Jobs are the opportunities your school shared with you. Start there.
  • Check the schedule and location first, and only apply to ones you can actually make.
  • Tap the bookmark to save one for later.
3Apply
Open the job→Apply Now
  • Answer “Why should we hire you?” in a few sentences: what interests you, one example of a skill, what you want to learn, and when you're available.
  • Check your spelling before you submit. This is the employer's first impression of you.
  • After you apply, the button changes to Applied. Tap View application status to follow along.
4Interviews and messages
Speech bubble (top right)
  • The School tab has messages from your school team. The Jobs tab has messages from employers, including interview details.
  • Reply within one school day, and tell your coordinator when you have an interview.
  • Keep conversations with employers in Starteryou, not by personal text or social media.
5Accept your offer
My Program→Applications→View Offer→Accept Offer
  • Each application shows its status, like Applied or Hired. When you get an offer, open it here.
  • Tap Confirm & Accept to finish.
  • Let the other employers you applied to know you've accepted a position.

Before your first day, check with your coordinator that any forms your school needs are turned in.

6Log your hours (every shift)
My Program→Work Logs→Log Hours
  • Select your placement, choose One day, and tap the day you worked on the calendar. Then enter hours and minutes.
  • Under “What did you work on?”, write one or two sentences about your day.
  • Log the same day you work. New entries show Pending until your supervisor verifies them.
  • Follow your school's rules for how many hours you can log.
7Check your hours
My Program→Work Logs
  • See your total, verified, pending and rejected hours at a glance. Use the tabs to filter your entries.
  • You can edit an entry with the pencil while it's still Pending.
  • If an entry is rejected, message your supervisor to find out why, then tell your coordinator.
8Evaluations
My Program→Evaluations
  • Supervisor Evaluations show how your supervisor rated your work.
  • When your school asks for a self-evaluation, tap New Self-Evaluation under Self-Evaluations.
9Found a job on your own?
My Program→Report a Placement I Found
  • Talk to your coordinator first. Your school may need to approve the site.
  • Enter the details and tap Send Invite so your supervisor can join Starteryou and verify your hours.

Remember

  • Late or absent? Message your supervisor and tell your coordinator before your shift starts.
  • Feel unsafe? Leave and contact your school right away.
  • Show up ready. Be on time, dress for the workplace, and ask questions.
